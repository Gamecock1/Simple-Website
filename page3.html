Our Story • Our Mission

A little care. A lasting impact.

At Pawmetto Lifeline, we believe every animal deserves love, safety, and the opportunity to find a forever home. Our mission is to support animals and the people who care for them throughout South Carolina's Midlands.

Through adoption, fostering, wellness services, and community involvement, we hope to make a difference one paw at a time.

How It All Began

Our Origin Story

Pawmetto Lifeline began with a simple idea: no animal should have to go without love, care, or a safe place to call home. Our fictional founders noticed how many animals in their community needed help finding families and receiving basic wellness care.

Inspired to make a difference, they created Pawmetto Lifeline as a community-centered organization focused on helping pets and connecting them with caring people. What started as a small idea grew into a mission built around compassion, responsibility, and second chances.

Today, our goal is to help reduce pet overpopulation and animal suffering through adoption, fostering, spay and neuter services, and education. We believe that when a community comes together, every animal has a better chance at a brighter future.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I adopt a pet?

Browse available pets on our Services & Adoption page and contact our team to learn about the adoption process.

Can I volunteer or become a foster?

Yes! Community members can help by volunteering their time or providing temporary homes for animals waiting to be adopted.

What types of animals do you help?

Our project focuses on helping dogs and cats find safe, loving homes.

How can I support the organization?

You can help by adopting, fostering, volunteering, donating supplies, or sharing our mission with others.
